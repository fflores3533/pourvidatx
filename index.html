What Pour Vida does

She brings the bar. You bring the alcohol. Guests get the experience.

Pour Vida is a dry hire bar service. The client is solely responsible for providing the alcohol, and Pour Vida takes care of the rest: bartending service, tools, fresh ingredients, crafted drink service, and the polished details that make the bar feel intentional.

What does dry hire mean?

The client provides the alcohol. Pour Vida provides the bar service, tools, fresh ingredients, and service experience depending on the package.

What events does Pour Vida serve?

Weddings, birthdays, private parties, corporate events, and other celebrations in the DFW area.

Can non-alcoholic drinks be included?

Yes. Beer, wine, cocktails, and non-alcoholic options can all be discussed in the inquiry.

Does Pour Vida offer tastings?

Yes. Tastings are available with a $50 non-refundable deposit that can be applied after the proposal is accepted.
